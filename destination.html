Travelogue: Paris, France

Overview:

Paris, is a very famous city located in France. It serves as the capital of France and is well known for it's art, iconic landmarks, history, and culture. Visitors from all around the world are drawn to Paris for its romantic ambiance, muesuems, charming cafes, and beautiful architecture.

Must-See Spots:

  1. Eiffel Tower
  2. Louvre Museum
  3. Champs-Élysées
  4. Palace of Versailles
  5. Notre-Dame Cathedral

Visitor Tips:

  • Book accommodations in advance, especially during peak season.
  • Carry a reusable water bottle and wear comfortable walking shoes.(There will be lots of walking!)
  • Learn a few basic French phrase/words. (Trust me, it's worth it!)
  • Always say Bonjour (Hello) and Merci (Thank you) when entering or leaving a shop or restaurant.
  • Be respectful and HAVE FUN!
